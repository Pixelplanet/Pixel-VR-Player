## Pixel VR Player v1.13.1 — "Up next" every time

### 🚀 Launching
- The launch fix from v1.13.0 now works on every launch: SteamVR shows **Up next** with the
  Pixel VR Player artwork, then drops you straight into the player. In v1.13.0 the player
  connected to SteamVR too quickly, so the dashboard often missed the launch and stayed on
  **Resume Game**.

### 📦 Installing and removing
- **One command to install**, with a copy button on the
  [README](https://github.com/Pixelplanet/Pixel-VR-Player#quick-install-one-command):
  ```sh
  curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Pixelplanet/Pixel-VR-Player/main/tools/install.sh | sh
  ```
- Installing from a terminal now asks whether to restart Steam when that is needed, as the
  desktop installer already did.
- **Uninstall** closes Steam for a moment so the library entry really goes away, and takes
  the player out of SteamVR's app list. Settings, bookmarks, thumbnails and the vault are kept.

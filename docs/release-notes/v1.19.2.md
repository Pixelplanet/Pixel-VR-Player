## Pixel VR Player v1.19.2: correct aspect ratio for non-square pixels, cleaner screen edges and glow

### 📐 Aspect ratio
- **Videos with non-square pixels are shown in their real shape.** A file like 1920x720 flagged as 16:9 was
  shown at 22:9; anamorphic DVDs and similar files were stretched the same way. The player now reads the
  pixel aspect ratio from the file. ([#3](https://github.com/Pixelplanet/Pixel-VR-Player/issues/3))
- Thumbnails of such files show the whole picture (a wide frame was taken for side-by-side 3D and cut in half).
  Thumbnails made before stay as they are until the thumbnail cache is cleared.

### ✨ Screen glow
- **New setting: Glow Distance** (Close / Short / Medium / Wide) under Settings › Display & Cinema, next to
  the brightness. Wide is the look from before.
- **Films with black bars glow too:** the glow takes its colours from the picture, not from the bars that
  are cut away.
- A dark picture no longer puts a dark band over the glow of the screen next to it.

### 🖼 Screen edges
- **No more black lines along the edges of a screen:** above the top edge when looking up at a curved
  screen, along the top and bottom of films with removed black bars, and down one side of side-by-side 3D
  films.
- Where screens overlap, the edge of the front screen no longer shows the other screen's glow as a thin gap.
- **Black-bar removal also works for files in the vault.** The measurement stays in memory and is not
  written to disk.

### 🖥 Multi-screen
- **A third screen makes "two below, one above"** by default (the new screen on top), also in the screen
  picker's preview. Three in a row is still in Arrange Screens, and stays your choice for the session once
  picked.

## Pixel VR Player v1.9.0 — Multiple screens

Watch up to **three videos at once**, each on its own screen, arranged around you as one
wall. Turn it on in **Settings › Display & Cinema › Multiple Screens**.

### 🖥️ Multiple screens
- **Up to 3 screens** playing at the same time, including 3D side-by-side and 4K videos.
  Each screen keeps its own format (2D / 3D), position in the video and curvature.
- **Pick where a video plays:** when something is already playing, opening a file asks
  *"Play on which screen?"* and shows your screens **live, laid out exactly as they are
  around you**. Point at a screen to replace it, or at a **+** tile to add a new screen in
  the spot it will appear.
- **Point and pull the trigger on a screen to select it.** The playback controls then
  control that screen.
- **Screen controls in the dock:** a new row under the playback controls with
  **Mute / Unmute**, **Unlock screens**, **Show / Hide borders**, **Arrange** and
  **Close screen** for the selected screen.
- **Audio:** by default you hear only the selected screen; switch to hearing all screens
  mixed in **Settings › Display & Cinema › Screen Audio**. Each screen can also be muted
  on its own.

### 🧩 Arranging screens
- Screens start as one **edge-to-edge side-by-side wall**. Making it bigger or moving it
  moves all screens together.
- **Arrange menu** with a live preview of the result:
  - Layouts: **Side by side**, **Two below, one above**, **Stacked** and
    **Main + sides** (one big screen with smaller ones beside it).
  - **Swap arrows** between neighbouring screens in the preview: click one to swap
    those two screens.
  - **Snap edges:** screens touch edge to edge, or keep a small gap.
  - **Style:** **Curved** (the wall wraps around you as one continuous curve) or
    **Flat wall** (all screens in one plane, facing the same way).
  - **Apply** moves the real screens to exactly what the preview shows.
- With 3 screens side by side, the **middle screen always stays centred** in front of you,
  in line with the controls, even if the side videos are wider or narrower.
- New screens **join the layout you are using** in their next position.
- **Unlock screens** to grab them with the laser and hold the trigger to move them;
  thumbstick up/down changes the distance, left/right the size. A snapped wall moves and
  resizes as a whole.
- **Recenter** brings the whole arrangement back in front of you.

### ✨ Details
- Thin borders mark the screens when more than one is visible (hide them with the new
  border button); the selected screen's border uses the theme colour.
- The ambient glow around the selected screen no longer spills onto other screens.
- Curved screens now bend on a true circle for every aspect ratio (wide cinema and 3D
  side-by-side videos looked slightly squashed before).

### ⚙️ Notes
- With **Multiple Screens** off (the default), the player works exactly as before with a
  single screen.
- 180° / 360° videos always play alone on the main screen; other screens pause while one
  is playing.
- The hardware decoder has a limit: if another video would not fit, the player tells you
  to close a screen first. Three 1080p videos, or a 4K video plus others, have been
  tested to work.

## Pixel VR Player v1.19.3: screen size, one Delete everywhere, many fixes

### 🐞 Fixes first
- **Settings › Tracks › Video Track** shows its list again (it was empty in v1.19.1 and v1.19.2).
- **DivX files play:** `.divx` and other older video files (`.xvid`, `.mpeg`, `.vob`, `.3gp`, `.ogv`, ...) are
  now recognised.
- **Black bars are removed on more films:** one frame with more black on one side (end credits, a dark sky)
  no longer cancels the removal for the whole film.
- **Music next to films:** a music screen could keep the shape of the film that played there before, slide
  under its neighbour and lose the swap arrow in Arrange Screens.
- **A dark slab behind a browser or Plex window** in a layout is gone, and a lower window (a page without a
  toolbar next to a browser) now sits against the row above instead of leaving a gap.
- Deleting a vault file, or moving it out of the vault, removes its thumbnail right away.
- Thumbnails of very wide videos made before v1.19.2 are made again once.

### 📏 Screen size, apart from distance
- **Hold a bumper and push the stick up / down** to change the screen's size; the stick alone still moves it
  nearer and farther. Works for a single screen, for a free-placed screen, and for a whole snapped layout
  (the screens grow together and keep their relative sizes).
- **Settings › Display & Cinema › Screen Size:** Standard, Large, Cinema (very big and further away) and
  Original Pixels (one video pixel per display pixel; meant for 720p and 1080p).
- The button labels show "Hold: Size" on the bumpers.

### 🗑 Delete, the same everywhere
- Deleting a **shortcut** or a **plugin** asks in the same dialog as deleting a file, instead of "press
  again". Both lists have a red **Delete** button, and a shortcut's and a plugin's own page have a red
  Delete row.
- The dialog's question wraps onto several lines instead of being cut off. A click on the dialog beside its
  buttons does nothing; a click outside cancels.
- **Download Folder** asks in the same dialog too, with the folder's size.

### ⚙ Settings and menus
- **Plugin settings show what they are:** switches for on / off, a pill for choices, buttons for actions and
  text fields.
- Long **track and language lists**, the **plugin list** and the **plugin store** turn pages.
- **List view:** readable column captions with their own row, and wider columns for resolution and duration.
- **Hover rumble** on keyboard keys, dialog buttons, download cards, path parts and more.
- The **browser toolbar** uses your accent and text colour.
- One scrollbar look in the file browser, Downloads and the music queue.
- The dock's **Recenter** pill is gone (the X button recenters); its place is kept for a future button.

### 🧰 Under the hood
- A plain message in the log, and a warning from the installer, when SteamVR's runtime is not set up.
- The uninstaller cleans up after itself when it is interrupted.

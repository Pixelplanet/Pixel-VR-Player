## Pixel VR Player v1.22.0: ready for SteamOS 0.5.10, Focus for close-ups, share logins

### 🎞 SteamOS 0.5.10 and the video decoder
- **8K videos play again on SteamOS 0.5.10.** The update brought a new video decoder driver that takes fewer
  picture buffers. 8192x4096 and 7200x3600 videos (8- and 10-bit) now start at once again, without a black
  screen or a delay.
- **No more lock-ups while downloading.** A download started while a video was open kept that video's decoder
  session busy until the download ended, so every later 8K video was refused. On SteamOS 0.5.10 that could freeze
  the headset until SteamVR restarted. Nothing the player starts holds the decoder any more.
- **A decoder that won't start now says so** ("Cannot play: …"). The player no longer retries endlessly in the
  background, and it tries once more with fewer buffers before giving up.
- **Free Decoder:** when Steam's own menus keep the headset's video decoder busy (a paused or hidden video in
  Steam), a 4K or larger video would play in software and stutter. The player now offers to free the decoder
  (Free Decoder / Play Anyway); Steam restarts the helper it ends.
- **A file that ends before its length says so** ("Playback ended: the file ends early …"). Short reads in the
  middle of a file are waited out instead of ending playback.
- **Downloads:** they are written to a `.part` file and checked against the server's size before they count as
  done. Quitting no longer waits for a running download.

### 🔭 Focus for 180 / 360 close-ups
- **New: Focus.** In a 3D 180° / 360° video, hold a grip (a light squeeze is enough) and push a stick up or down.
  Up moves the picture farther away, which makes very close scenes much easier on the eyes. It starts at 0 with
  every video.
- While you change Focus or zoom, the title shows both, e.g. "Zoom 70 % · Focus +6°".
- **Sharper highlights in 180 / 360:** small bright highlights no longer sparkle. Where the video has more detail
  than the headset shows (8K, or zoomed out), each screen pixel now averages the part of the video it covers.

### 🔐 Network shares
- **Shares that need a login ask for it:** a Sign In dialog opens right where it is needed: when browsing, or
  when a video can't open because its share's password changed. After you sign in, the video plays.
  - "Save password" works per share. Without it the password is kept in memory only, until the player quits.
  - A refused password is never sent again on its own, so a server doesn't lock the account.
- **Shares can be kept in the vault:** a share marked as a vault share stays out of sight while the vault is locked,
  and downloads from it go into the vault, encrypted.
- Messages about a file that can't open name the reason first.

### 🐞 Fixes
- Paths inside the vault never show up in the log; the log can no longer grow by megabytes a second.
- A child program started by the player (downloads, the browser screen, plugins) no longer inherits its open
  files.

### ⚠ Notes
- Focus's light grip uses the action "Laser Hand (Analog)". The default bindings include it. If you made your own
  SteamVR bindings for the player, bind it to the grip's value, or use the full grip press, which works either way.
- AV1 videos still decode on the CPU (up to 4K60 and 8K30 run fine). SteamOS 0.5.10's decoder can also decode AV1
  in hardware; support for that is planned.

## Pixel VR Player v1.20.0: subtitles, Skip Intro, one-handed controls

### 💬 Subtitles
- **Text subtitles are shown:** tracks inside the video and `.srt` files next to it. Pick one in the dock's
  track list (the Audio pill); switching needs no reload.
- The **track list** shows what each row is: a speaker for audio, a caption box for subtitles, a screen for
  video, and a tick on the one in use. Subtitle rows say what they are in plain words (full, foreign-language
  parts only, with sound descriptions, commentary, from a file), with a sentence about the selected row.
- **Plex:** the subtitle track chosen on the Plex page is taken over.
- Picture subtitles (Blu-ray / DVD) are listed but cannot be shown yet. Subtitles are drawn on flat screens,
  not in 360 / 180.

### ⏭ Skip Intro / Skip Credits
- For **Plex**, and for Jellyfin servers that provide media segments: a pill at the lower right of the screen
  while the intro or the credits play. Credits that run to the end of an episode go on to the next one.
- **Point and pull the trigger, or tap the right bumper.** The tap counts when the bumper is let go, and never
  after it was held to resize or pan.

### 🎮 One-handed controls
- **Hold a bumper and this hand's buttons act as the other hand's:** with the right bumper, A / B / X / Y work
  as D-pad down / right / left / up; with the left bumper, the D-pad works as those four. It follows whatever
  is bound in SteamVR.
- **Previous / Next** (hub tabs, music tracks) now happen when the bumper is let go, so holding it for the
  above does not turn a tab first.
- **No accidental scrubbing:** the direction a stick push starts in holds until the stick is back in the
  middle. Moving or resizing the screen no longer scrubs from a little sideways drift, and a scrub no longer
  moves the screen. A firm push (60 %) in the other direction still gets through.

### 🖥 Screens
- **Single-screen mode: drag the video with the trigger.** There is no lock there any more. Recenter, or a
  change of size, distance or curve in Settings, puts it back in front. A click without a drag hides the
  controls as before.
- **Multi-screen:** unlocking still shows the screen numbers, but the numbers button can now switch them off
  while unlocked.
- The **laser's end circle** stays readable on big screens far away (it was a white speck).

### ⬛ Black bars
- **Films on network shares** (SMB, SFTP, WebDAV) are measured too; before, their bars always stayed.
- **Thin bars** are cut (films cropped to 1920x808 with a couple of black rows left), and bars that differ by
  a few rows above and below are cut evenly.
- 4K films with lossless audio are measured correctly.

### 🐞 Fixes and small things
- The **blurred picture behind the hub** reaches the screen's edge; a strip of the sharp video no longer
  shows beside it.
- The **track list** is wider, so its text fits.
- **YouTube Music** is a built-in shortcut (switched off by default; turn it on in Settings › Shortcuts).
- `.srt` files: lines starting with full-width punctuation keep their first character, a `.hi.srt` file is
  Hindi and not "with sound descriptions", and files next to `.ts` videos are in sync.

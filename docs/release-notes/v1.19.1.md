## Pixel VR Player v1.19.1: a real uninstaller, unified 360/180 sticks, smoother loading

### 🗑 Uninstaller
- **Double-click to uninstall:** the `PixelVRPlayer` folder now has an **Uninstall Pixel VR Player** icon and an
  `uninstall.sh`. It asks first, then removes the app and its Steam library entry. Settings, your Funscripts
  folder and the vault are kept; with files in the vault it asks whether to keep it encrypted or decrypt it.
- **Works from Desktop Mode on the Steam Frame:** removing the library entry needs Steam closed for a moment,
  which also closes Desktop Mode. The uninstaller now finishes that step by itself after the window is gone.
  Before, an uninstall started from Desktop Mode stopped half way and left the library entry behind.

### 🎮 Controls
- **One stick layout for 360 / 180 video:** right stick left / right scrubs, up / down zooms; left stick
  left / right skips 30 s; hold a bumper and move a stick to pan and tilt.
- **Three new settings (off by default):** scrub, zoom and recenter without bringing the controls back up.
  In 360 / 180, zoom and pan / tilt never wake hidden controls.
- **Button labels:** the bumper has a label, and the stick labels change to Pan / Tilt while it is held.
  Labels are now hidden by the controller when they are behind it, and the lines to the trigger, bumper and
  grip reach the button.

### 🖥 Interface
- **Opening a video no longer freezes the player:** files from a network share (or a slow card) load in the
  background with a "Loading..." screen.
- **Close button** on the main menu, and the **Settings path is clickable** (click a parent page to go back).
- **Download Folder** has its own page: one choice per line with the free space of each.
- **Tracks:** audio, video and subtitle tracks open as a list; embedded cover art is no longer offered as a
  video track.
- Sliders can be clicked at the step you want; the downloads list scrolls; the screen buttons sit centred
  under Play.
- Deleting a network share from its editor uses the same confirmation as everywhere else.
- **Incognito** applies to the full browser only, and its label says so.

### 🔐 Vault and SD card
- **Moving the vault to a slow SD card** no longer stalls the player or locks the vault mid-move; progress
  shows speed and time left.
- **Auto-Lock on Headset Removal** off is respected: the vault no longer locks itself when you take the
  headset off.
- Vault files show the right format badge (a random file name containing "3d" was shown as 3D SBS).
- Notices at the bottom of the file browser are no longer cut off by the progress line.

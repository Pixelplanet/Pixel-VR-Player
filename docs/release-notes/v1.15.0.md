## Pixel VR Player v1.15.0: Jellyfin, TV series, your own shortcuts

### 🍿 Jellyfin
- **New Jellyfin shortcut.** Browse your library in Jellyfin's own web pages. Pressing Play hands the
  original file to Pixel VR Player, so it plays with hardware decoding, including 4K HEVC 10-bit and
  DTS / TrueHD audio. Only files the player can't decode are converted by Jellyfin.
- Your server's address is asked for the first time you open the shortcut. You can also fill it in
  beforehand under **Settings › UI & Appearance › Shortcuts**; `192.168.1.10:8096` is enough.
- Jellyfin always knows where you are: resume points, "Continue watching" and watched marks stay
  correct.
- The audio track you pick on the Jellyfin page plays in the player. Without a choice, Jellyfin's
  default for your language is used.
- **Downloads** from Jellyfin go into the player's Downloads window with the film's real file name,
  progress, pause and cancel.
- Press **Stop** to return to the same Jellyfin page.

### 📺 TV series (Plex and Jellyfin)
- **The next episode starts by itself** when one ends. After the last one you are back on the page
  right away.
- **Previous / next episode on the screen:** point the laser at a playing episode and a round arrow
  appears at its left and right edge. The episode you leave keeps its progress.
- **The episode's title shows on the screen** as it starts, for example "Futurama · S6 E13 · The
  Futurama Holiday Spectacular".
- Plex: the audio track you picked in Plex now plays in the player too.

### ⭐ Shortcuts, your way
- **A new shortcut list** under **Settings › UI & Appearance › Shortcuts**. Each line has:
  - a handle to drag it into a new place
  - an on / off switch for the rail
  - its name and address
  - **Edit**, to change the name, the address, and whether it opens as a page only or the full browser
  - **Delete**, which asks once more before it deletes
- **Add your own shortcuts** (up to 10), e.g. your Jellyfin server or any website.
- The rail shows the first 5 shortcuts that are switched on, in the list's order. Your current
  shortcuts carry over.

### ⚡ Faster and smoother
- **Downloads from Plex, Jellyfin and WebDAV are up to about twice as fast.**
- **No more freezing when a server is slow to start a video** (for example while its disk spins up).
  "Waiting for Jellyfin / Plex to start the video..." shows on the screen instead.
- **The end of a streamed video is noticed at once.** It used to take about 15-20 s before the player
  went on.
- **Scrubbing Plex and Jellyfin videos** now shows the preview picture above the timeline while you
  drag.

### 🛠 Other changes
- **No more floating text in front of you.** Messages appear on the screen they concern, or right
  above the control panel.
- The **hide controls** button has its own icon (it looked like Close Screen before).
- The return hint now says "Press Stop to return to Plex / Jellyfin". B hides the controls.
- Fixed: a crash that could happen with some 7.1 DTS-HD audio after resuming a film.

> [!NOTE]
> Subtitles can't be chosen in the player yet. That and skip intro / credits for Plex and Jellyfin
> are next on the list.

## Pixel VR Player v1.10.0 — Web, YouTube and music

Browse the web and watch YouTube on any screen, play your music with a real playlist,
and set the volume of every screen on its own.

### 🌐 YouTube and a full web browser
- Two new tabs in the hub: **YouTube** opens youtube.com on a screen, **Browser** opens a
  full web browser. Both are screens like any video: pick where they go, move them,
  resize them, put them in an arrangement, and open several at once.
- **The full browser** has tabs (links that open new windows become tabs), back /
  forward / reload / home, an **address bar** (type a web address or a search),
  your **Chromium bookmarks** (bookmarks bar and menu; the ☆ star adds or removes the
  current page) and a **fullscreen button**. In fullscreen, point at the top edge to
  bring the toolbar back. Downloads go to your Downloads folder.
- **The laser is the mouse:** pull the trigger to click, thumbstick up/down scrolls,
  **B** goes back.
- **Typing:** clicking into a text field (a search box, a sign-in form, the address bar)
  opens the keyboard, which types straight into the page. A **KEYBOARD** button in the
  dock opens it by hand.
- **The dock controls the page's video:** play / pause, ±10 s, the timeline and the
  thumbsticks work for YouTube and other sites like they do for your own files.
- **Livestreams:** the timeline covers what you can rewind, with **● LIVE** at the right
  end (red at the live edge, grey when you are behind). Click it to jump back to live.
- **Private Browsing (Incognito)** in *Settings › Vault & Privacy*: new browser screens
  keep no history or cookies.
- Page pop-ups (alerts, "leave this page?") and dropdown menus on forms open as menus
  over the page.
- Uses the **Chromium you install from Discover** (Flathub). If you signed in to sites in
  desktop Chromium, you are signed in here too (while desktop Chromium is closed).

### 🎵 Music
- **Audio files play:** MP3, FLAC, M4A, AAC, OGG, Opus, WAV and more. The screen shows the
  album cover (or a music note), the title, artist and album.
- **Playlist:** opening a track queues the whole folder. Tracks play one after another,
  with **shuffle** and **repeat** (off / all / one), previous / next, and the queue shown
  next to the cover. Click a track to play it; point at the list and use the thumbstick
  to scroll.
- Folders now list tracks separately from videos, e.g. "3 videos, 8 tracks".

### 🔊 Volume and audio
- **VOLUME** in the dock for every screen (videos, music and web pages): point at it and
  push the thumbstick left / right, click it to mute. This works on top of the headset's
  own volume.
- With several screens, **every screen now plays its own sound** from the start. The new
  **Audio follows selection** button in the screen row mutes the others and plays only
  the screen you select. (The old *Screen Audio* setting is gone.)
- **Pause When Headset Is Removed** in *Settings › Display & Cinema* (on by default),
  with **Pause Livestreams Too** (off by default: livestreams keep running).

### 🎬 Video
- **AV1 videos** play (decoded on the CPU; 4K at 25–30 fps runs smoothly).
- **3D without a hint in the file name:** side-by-side and top-bottom 3D (and 180° / 360°)
  are recognised from the file's own metadata, or by comparing the two halves of the
  picture.
- **Your manual format choice and resume position survive renaming or moving a file.**
- A file that can't be played now says why in the file browser instead of doing nothing.

### ⌨️ Keyboard
- **German keyboard layout** (QWERTZ with ä ö ü ß) in *Settings › Controls & Input ›
  Keyboard Layout*.
- The delete key shows a proper backspace symbol.

### 🛠️ Fixes
- Screen arrangements placed above or below eye level now stay edge to edge instead of
  the screens tilting apart.
- Putting something new on a screen of an arrangement keeps the screen in its place.
- Adjusting volume or curvature with the thumbstick no longer also seeks in the video.
- Folder downloads in the queue no longer show more than 100 %.
- Old thumbnails of deleted files are cleaned up automatically.
- Much less log spam with only one controller connected.

### ⚙️ Notes
- YouTube and the web browser need **Chromium from Discover**
  (`org.chromium.Chromium`). Without it, the app tells you so when you open one.
- Video inside web pages is decoded on the CPU (Chromium has no hardware decoder for this
  device). One 4K or two 1080p / 1440p web videos are fine; more can stutter.

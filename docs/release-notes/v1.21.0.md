## Pixel VR Player v1.21.0: every subtitle, one controller, one look

### 💬 Subtitles, all of them
- **Picture subtitles:** Blu-ray (PGS), DVD and DVB tracks are shown, where the track places them. On a film
  whose black bars are cut, a line is moved up into the picture.
- **Every script and full styling:** text subtitles are drawn by libass. Cyrillic, Greek, Chinese, Japanese,
  Korean, Arabic, Hebrew, Thai and others show correctly, and `.ass` / `.ssa` keep their fonts, colours and
  positions.
- **Subtitles return after a jump:** the line that should be on screen is back at once after a seek.
- **3D subtitle tracks** (side by side and top / bottom) give each eye its own copy, so nothing is doubled.
- **In 360 / 180:** the subtitle floats below the centre of view and follows your head gently.
- **More places:** files next to a video on a network share (SMB, SFTP, WebDAV) or next to a vault video,
  external subtitles from Plex and Jellyfin, and Jellyfin's chosen track. Files next to a local video can be
  `.srt`, `.ass`, `.ssa`, `.vtt`, `.sup` or `.idx`.
- **Preferred subtitles:** Settings › Playback & Tracks › Subtitles (Off / Foreign parts only / Always) and
  Subtitle Language. A track picked by hand for a film is remembered for that film.
- A track with only a few lines is listed as "foreign-language parts only", with its line count.

### 🎮 Everything with the controller
- **Files:** one highlight, whoever moved last wins: the D-pad / stick or the laser. A opens what is
  highlighted. Left from the first column goes to the rail, Up from the top row to the header (Sort, View,
  + Folder).
- **Options (Y) on a file** starts with Play. **Move / Copy:** walk to the target folder and press Y; "Move
  here" is already highlighted. Cancelling says that nothing was moved.
- **Settings:** Left / Right change sliders and choices both ways; every section, the colour picker and the
  list pages work with the buttons. A hidden "Debug Statistics" row is visible again.
- **Dialogs:** B is one step back everywhere, side-by-side buttons work with Left / Right, the keyboard has a
  D-pad focus, and a half-filled share form asks before it is thrown away. A missed trigger pull no longer
  runs the last highlighted action.
- **Downloads:** the cards work with the buttons, and Cancel / Remove ask first.
- **Bumper taps on a video:** right = Skip Intro / Credits while that pill shows, otherwise the next episode
  (Plex / Jellyfin) or the next video in the folder; left = the previous one. Holding still resizes.
- **One-handed:** left bumper + View opens the Main Menu, right bumper + Menu shows or hides the controls, and
  while a bumper is held the button labels show what the buttons do then.
- Every button step gives a light tick; the dock no longer hides while you aim at it.
- **Reset Horizon** (360 / 180) also turns the view level and in front of you, and says so.

### 🎨 One look
- **Colour picker** for the text and the accent colour (saturation / brightness square, hue strip, today's
  colours as swatches, Reset).
- Five text sizes, four button heights, fixed corners and borders, one way to show hover, focus and selection.
- Delete is always the same red, done the same green, warnings the same amber; when your accent is close to
  one of them, those elements get a strong rim, and the picker tells you.
- Labels on accent-coloured buttons turn dark or light so any accent stays readable; secondary text has its
  own contrast floor.
- At large text sizes labels step down instead of being cut off; slider values make room for themselves.
- Dock tooltips only where they add something (volume level, the time readout, the round buttons).

### 🐞 Fixes
- **Some 180 / 360 videos stayed black** (7200x3600 8-bit HEVC): the player asked the decoder for more picture
  buffers than the Steam Frame's decoder accepts at that size. They play now.
- Failed Plex / Jellyfin or queue opens show a message instead of nothing.

### ⚠ Note
- Text subtitles need libass, which SteamOS already has (it comes with SteamOS's own FFmpeg).

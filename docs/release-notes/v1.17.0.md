## Pixel VR Player v1.17.0: frame-sequential 3D, no more black bars

### 🎞️ Frame-sequential 3D (FS3D)
- **New 3D format.** FS3D films store the left and right picture one after the other (for example 48
  frames per second = 24 per eye at full resolution). The player pairs them and shows each eye its own
  picture at full HD, with hardware decoding.
- **Recognised automatically** from the MKV 3D tag, from the marking inside the H.264 / H.265 stream
  (also in MP4 files), or from "FS3D" in the file name. Seeking never swaps the eyes.
- Files without any marking: choose **3D FS** in the format menu. The choice is remembered per file.

### ⬛ Black bars removed
- Films with black bars encoded above and below the picture (typical for cinema-format films) now play
  **without the bars**: the screen takes the film's real shape, and arranged screens sit next to each
  other without gaps.
- The bars are measured in the background on frames spread across the film; only what every bright frame
  shows as black is left out, so dark scenes can never cut into the picture. Works for 2D and side-by-side
  3D, also 4K HDR.
- Can be switched off under **Settings › Display & Cinema › Remove Black Bars**.

### Format menu
- Every format now shows its short name with what it stands for: 2D, 3D SBS, 3D TB, 3D FS, VR180,
  VR180 3D, VR360, VR360 3D, VR360 SBS. VR180 and VR360 are now also available in 2D.
- The format pill under the timeline uses the same short names.
- The format menu in the file browser offers the same choices (its 360° entry did nothing before).

### Fixes
- The launcher no longer fails to start the player after the logs moved into the PixelVRPlayer folder.
- An old install log in the home folder is moved into PixelVRPlayer/logs during updates.
